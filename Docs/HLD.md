# Repaired · One-page HLD

**One-line pitch:** Play as a village repairer whose search for parts uncovers the story behind a mechanical bird, helping a grieving mother decide whether to use the shop’s magic for one final meeting with her daughter.

**Genre · view · platform:** Narrative puzzle adventure · 2D side view · Windows PC.

## Target experience
Warmth, curiosity, and satisfaction from solving practical puzzles gradually develop into an emotional understanding of the characters. Discoveries should feel meaningful because they change how the player and the mother understand the object.

## Core loop
Explore → investigate → solve → report. Each completed puzzle provides a repair component or evidence that unlocks new conversations and leads.

## Win and lose conditions
- **Win:** Complete the investigation, obtain the required repair parts, and fulfill Mara’s decision to take the bird home unfinished, reaching the bakery epilogue.
- **Lose:** No death or permanent game over. Incorrect codes or item choices provide feedback and allow retries without losing progress or items.
- There is no time limit. Deferring the final repair is the story’s intended conclusion.

## Controls
A/D or left/right arrows to walk; E to interact, talk, or enter doorways; mouse to select dialogue, inventory items, and interaction targets; keyboard to enter numbers; Esc to pause.

## Must-have features
- Main menu, pause menu, and ending screen with replay.
- Horizontal movement, collisions, and transitions between the village street and five small interiors.
- Object inspection, a small inventory, item use, and a notebook retaining clues.
- Authored dialogue that updates when relevant evidence is discovered.
- Five practical puzzle sequences: diagnose the bird, measure a replacement linkage, select a spring, trace a parcel using its receipt, and assemble the bird’s stand.
- Clear objectives, puzzle feedback, and retries without losing items.
- One complete customer story, Mara’s final decision, and a bakery epilogue.

## Look and sound
Warm, stylized 2D environments with readable objects and text-based dialogue. Gentle music and location-specific sounds support the village atmosphere; essential clues never rely on sound or color alone.

## Out of scope this semester
Jumping, combat, 3D movement, entering memory worlds, multiplayer, real-time deadlines, live AI-generated dialogue, and large branching stories. Additional customers, the Homecoming festival, shop decoration, and full voice acting are excluded.
